Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the organizers of Prabhu Deva LIVE — SIA Events, Chillax Live and Mathangi Entertainment Group (together, "we", "us" or "our") — handle information collected through this website, pdsiachillax.com (the "Site").

1. About This Site

This Site is an informational and promotional website for the Prabhu Deva LIVE dance concert at Gas South Arena, Atlanta, on August 2, 2026. We do not sell tickets on this Site. When you choose to buy tickets, you are redirected to Ticketmaster, and any information you provide there is governed by Ticketmaster's own privacy policy — not this one.

2. Information We Collect

We aim to keep data collection to a minimum. Depending on how you use the Site, we may collect:

  • Information you give us voluntarily. If you submit the sponsorship enquiry form or email/call us, we collect the details you provide — such as your name, email address, phone number, company/brand name and your message.
  • Usage and device data. When you visit the Site, standard analytics data may be collected automatically, such as your approximate location, browser type, device, pages viewed and how you arrived at the Site.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. We use cookies and tags (see below) to understand how the Site is used and to improve it.

We do not collect payment or credit card information on this Site — all ticket payments are processed by Ticketmaster.

3. How We Use Information

  • To respond to your enquiries, sponsorship requests and messages.
  • To operate, maintain and improve the Site and understand how visitors use it.
  • To send you information you have requested, such as sponsorship or partnership materials.
  • To keep the Site secure and to comply with applicable law.

4. Analytics & Cookies

This Site uses Google Tag Manager and related analytics services to measure traffic and performance. These services may set cookies and collect anonymized usage data. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; some parts of the Site may not function as intended if cookies are disabled. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share information only:

  • With trusted service providers who help us operate the Site and analytics (such as Google).
  • With our co-organizers and partners for the purpose of responding to your enquiry.
  • When required by law, legal process, or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

When you click through to Ticketmaster or any other third-party site, that third party's own privacy practices apply.

6. Data Retention

We keep enquiry and contact information only for as long as needed to respond to you and for our legitimate business records, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

7. Your Choices & Rights

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, to request corrections, or to request that we delete your information. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws. To make a request, email us at chillaxsia@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

This Site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Third-Party Links

The Site contains links to third-party websites, including Ticketmaster, sponsors and social media. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to review their policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your information, please reach out:
